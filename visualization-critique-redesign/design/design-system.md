# Design system

- **Intent:** analytical, calm, editorial; dense enough for serious comparison without feeling like a control room.
- **Type:** Manrope for display and DM Sans for interface/body copy; system sans-serif fallback.
- **Surfaces:** warm white cards on a pale mineral background, 1 px cool-grey borders, restrained shadow.
- **Color:** carrier color encodes energy type. Dark slate is reserved for conversion nodes. Grey identifies sinks and explicit balance adjustments.
- **Interaction:** selection is persistent and reversible. Related paths stay saturated; unrelated paths recede to 8% opacity. Hover supplies exact values, while the side panel preserves context.
- **Motion:** year playback uses discrete 1.3-second steps. Reduced-motion settings remove transitions.
- **Layout:** visualization and explanation remain side by side on wide screens. At tablet size, details move below. On mobile, the complex Sankey scrolls horizontally rather than collapsing into illegibility.
